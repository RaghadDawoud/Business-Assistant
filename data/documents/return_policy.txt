COMPANY INFORMATION

Company Name: X Fashion
Industry: Fashion and Retail
Head Office: Jeddah, Saudi Arabia
Business Type: E-commerce and physical retail
Primary Market: Saudi Arabia

DEPARTMENTS

The company has the following departments:

Sales: Responsible for sales operations, customer orders, and revenue targets.

Marketing: Responsible for digital campaigns, social media, promotions, and brand awareness.

Operations: Responsible for inventory, order fulfillment, logistics, and delivery coordination.

Customer Service: Handles customer inquiries, complaints, returns, exchanges, and order issues.

Finance: Responsible for financial reporting, invoices, payments, budgets, and revenue tracking.

Human Resources: Responsible for employee records, recruitment, leave requests, and company policies.


WORKING HOURS

The standard working hours for office employees are Sunday to Thursday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM.

The company headquarters is located in Jeddah, Saudi Arabia.

Some operational and retail employees may follow different schedules depending on their department and location.


LEAVE POLICY

Employees should submit annual leave requests through the HR system at least 7 days before the requested start date whenever possible.

Leave requests are subject to manager approval and operational requirements.

Emergency leave should be reported to the employee's direct manager and HR as soon as possible.


REMOTE WORK POLICY

Employees may work remotely only when their department and manager allow it.

Employees working remotely are expected to remain available during their scheduled working hours and attend required meetings.

Sensitive company information should not be accessed from unsecured public networks.


EXPENSE POLICY

Business-related expenses must be submitted through the company expense system.

Employees should keep the original invoice or receipt for business expenses.

Expenses above 1,000 SAR require manager approval before reimbursement.

Personal expenses are not eligible for reimbursement.


PURCHASING PROCEDURE

Employees requesting equipment, software, or other business purchases should submit a purchase request through the procurement system.

Requests above 5,000 SAR require department manager approval.

Requests above 20,000 SAR require additional Finance approval.

Procurement should be contacted if an employee needs assistance with an existing purchase request.


IT SUPPORT

Employees should contact the IT Support team for issues related to company laptops, accounts, software, network access, or other technical problems.

Employees should not share their company passwords with other employees or external parties.

Suspicious emails, links, or attachments should be reported to IT Security.


MEETING ROOMS

Meeting rooms can be reserved through the company's internal calendar system.

Employees should cancel reservations if a meeting is no longer required.

Meeting rooms should be left clean and ready for the next meeting.


CONTACT INFORMATION

HR handles employee-related questions, leave requests, and employment policies.

Finance handles invoices, reimbursements, and financial questions.

IT Support handles technical issues and system access.

Procurement handles purchasing requests and supplier-related questions.

Operations handles logistics, inventory, and order fulfillment questions.
